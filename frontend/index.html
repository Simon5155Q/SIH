Legal Framework

Enforced Under The Legal Metrology Act, 2009

Every instrument used in transaction, industrial measurement, or protection of human life is strictly governed under central statutory mandates. Unverified instruments are punishable by confiscation and statutory fines.

Section 24: Mandatory Verification & Stamping Every person possessing an unverified measuring tool for trade commits an offence under Section 33.
The Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011 Specifies precise maximum permissible error limits (MPE) and testing methodologies for classes I through IV.
